## Endpoint Style

Use camelCase for all Platform API resource identifiers in path segments. When defining a new resource, choose a concise plural noun in camelCase and keep that spelling consistent across collection and item routes.

Sample path snippets:
- `/v1/buildArtifacts`
- `/v1/deploymentTargets/{deploymentTargetId}`
- `/v1/serviceAccounts/{serviceAccountId}/accessTokens`
