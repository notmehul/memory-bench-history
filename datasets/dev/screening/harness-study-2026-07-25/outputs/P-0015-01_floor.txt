## Company Standup: Current Setup

We run one company-wide async standup every weekday in `#company-standup`.

**When:** Post by `10:15 AM` in your local time zone, Monday through Friday. If you are out of office, post your status before the end of your previous working day.

**Format:** Keep it to `3 bullets` and `5 lines max`.
- `Yesterday:` what you finished or moved forward
- `Today:` what you are working on next
- `Blockers:` anything slowing you down or needing help

**If blocked:** If a blocker has been open for more than `24 hours`, tag the right owner directly and move follow-up into a thread or a quick call. Standup should surface work, not become the discussion itself.

**Expectation:** Everyone posts. Keep updates clear, specific, and skim-friendly so the whole company can track progress without extra coordination.
