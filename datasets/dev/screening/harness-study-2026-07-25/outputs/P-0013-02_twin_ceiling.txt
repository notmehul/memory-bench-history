# Urgent Platform Support Threads

For urgent Slack threads routed to platform support, the growth team should treat the thread as a priority support lane and post an initial reply within **4 hours**. Use that first response to acknowledge ownership, clarify the next step if needed, and keep the thread updated until the issue is handed off or resolved.

If you pick up an urgent thread, make ownership clear in the thread so coverage stays unambiguous and duplicate replies are avoided. When a resolution will take longer, reply within the 4-hour window with status, expected next step, and who is driving follow-through.
