# Strategic Priorities: How They Reach Leadership

To surface strategic priorities across the business, we use a single operating rhythm: each function brings its most important decisions, risks, and asks into a shared leadership review. This is the forum where cross-company priorities are aligned, tradeoffs are made, and executive support is assigned.

## Timing
- Teams submit updates by **12:00 PM local time every Monday**.
- Function leads review and consolidate inputs by **5:00 PM every Tuesday**.
- The cross-business leadership review runs **every Wednesday for 60 minutes**.
- Urgent issues that cannot wait for the weekly cycle should be escalated within **24 hours** through the relevant executive sponsor.

## Who Joins
- CEO and executive staff
- Function heads
- Finance, People, Legal, and Operations leads as needed
- The directly responsible team lead for any item under discussion

## What Teams Should Bring
Bring only the items that need leadership attention:
- The **top 3 strategic priorities** for the next **30-90 days**
- Decisions needed from leadership
- Cross-functional dependencies or blockers
- Material risks to delivery, revenue, customers, compliance, or hiring
- Key changes in plan, scope, timing, or resourcing
- Metrics that show current status and expected impact

Each item should fit on **one page or one slide** and include:
- Objective
- Owner
- Decision or support needed
- Target date
- Current status
- Risk level: **Low, Medium, or High**

## Standard
If a priority needs funding, headcount, sequencing changes, or executive resolution, bring it to this review. If it can be resolved within a team, keep it within the team. This keeps leadership focused on the highest-leverage decisions for the company.
