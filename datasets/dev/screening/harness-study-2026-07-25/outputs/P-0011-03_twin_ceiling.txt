# Growth Team Partner Incident Checklist

Use this checklist for any incident that materially affects a partner-facing growth workflow, integration, campaign, reporting feed, data delivery, or partner-visible SLA.

## 1. Confirm This Is Partner-Facing
Treat the issue as a partner incident if at least one of these is true:

- A partner cannot access, use, or trust a growth-owned workflow or deliverable.
- Partner data is delayed, incomplete, duplicated, or incorrect.
- A live campaign, launch, report, sync, or scheduled delivery is disrupted.
- A partner has already noticed the issue or is likely to notice before the next scheduled touchpoint.

If none of the above apply, handle it as an internal issue instead.

## 2. Open Comms Ownership Immediately
Assign one comms owner for the incident as soon as partner impact is confirmed.

The comms owner is responsible for:

- Sending the first outbound partner note.
- Posting every partner update until the incident is resolved or handed off.
- Keeping the incident timeline, affected partners list, and next update time current.

No additional approval is required for the comms owner to send the first acknowledgment if the message stays within the approved template and avoids speculation.

## 3. Deadline for the First Outbound Note
Send the first outbound note to each affected partner within **2 business hours** of confirming partner impact.

If partner impact is confirmed outside business hours, on a weekend, or on a holiday, the first outbound note may wait until the **next business day**, but it must be sent by **10:00 AM in the partner’s local time** or **within 1 business hour of the growth team starting coverage**, whichever comes first.

Do not wait for root cause, engineering confirmation, or leadership approval before sending the first acknowledgment.

## 4. Minimum Facts to Gather Before Sending
Before the first note, confirm only these facts:

- What is affected.
- Which partners are affected.
- When the issue started or when it was first detected.
- Current status: investigating, identified, mitigated, or resolved.
- When the next update will be sent.

If any detail is unknown, say it is under investigation. Do not guess.

## 5. Send the First Outbound Note
Send this message immediately once the minimum facts above are known:

**Subject:** Acknowledgment: issue affecting [partner-facing workflow]

Hello [Partner Name],

We’re aware of an issue affecting [brief description of workflow, report, campaign, sync, or deliverable]. Our team is actively investigating.

What we know right now:
- Impact: [plain-language description]
- Start time: [time, or “under investigation”]
- Status: [investigating / identified / mitigated]

We’ll send the next update by [time with time zone], even if we are still investigating.

If you have urgent operational needs in the meantime, reply here and we’ll coordinate directly.

Best,  
[Name]  
Growth Team

## 6. Update Cadence While the Incident Is Active
After the first outbound note, send partner updates on this cadence:

- **Severity High:** every **60 minutes**
- **Severity Medium:** every **4 business hours**
- **Severity Low:** by the **end of the same business day**, then **once per business day**

If a promised update time is approaching and there is no new information, still send an update confirming the issue remains active and giving the next update time.

## 7. Severity Guide for Growth Partner Incidents
Use these levels to set cadence:

- **High:** multiple partners blocked, launch-critical disruption, materially incorrect partner-visible data, or likely executive escalation.
- **Medium:** one or more partners degraded, delayed, or at moderate business risk, but with workaround or partial functionality.
- **Low:** minor degradation, limited scope, non-critical delay, or issue unlikely to change partner operations today.

If unsure, start at Medium until clarified.

## 8. What to Say and What Not to Say
Always include:

- Acknowledgment that the issue exists.
- Clear statement of current impact.
- Current status.
- Concrete next update time.
- Point of contact.

Do not include:

- Guesses about root cause.
- Blame on another team or vendor.
- Promises on resolution time unless confirmed.
- Unreviewed legal, security, or financial conclusions.

## 9. Partner List and Segmentation
Track all affected partners in one working list with:

- Partner name
- Primary contact
- Severity for that partner
- Time first notified
- Latest update sent
- Next update due
- Any partner-specific workaround or commitment

If impact differs by partner, tailor the message rather than sending one generic note to everyone.

## 10. Escalate Within Growth When Any of These Are True
Escalate to the growth lead immediately if:

- More than 3 partners are affected.
- The incident lasts more than 4 business hours.
- A launch, campaign, or scheduled delivery date is at risk.
- A partner requests executive involvement.
- The issue involves contractual reporting, billing-related metrics, or data accuracy concerns.

## 11. Resolution Note
When the incident is mitigated or resolved, send a closeout note within **2 business hours**:

**Subject:** Resolved: [partner-facing workflow issue]

Hello [Partner Name],

The issue affecting [workflow/deliverable] has been resolved as of [time with time zone].

Summary:
- Impact: [brief description]
- Resolution: [brief description]
- Follow-up: [optional next step, backfill, rerun, or monitoring note]

Please reply if you are still seeing any issues on your side.

Best,  
[Name]  
Growth Team

## 12. Aftercare
Within **1 business day** after resolution:

- Confirm all affected partners received the resolution note.
- Send any promised reruns, backfills, or corrected deliverables.
- Record the incident timeline, partner impact, and communication timestamps for future reference.
- Note any improvements needed to reduce repeat partner impact.
