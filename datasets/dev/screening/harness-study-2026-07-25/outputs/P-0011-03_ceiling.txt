# Growth Team Partner Incident Checklist

## Purpose
Use this checklist whenever an incident affects a partner or is reasonably likely to affect a partner. The goal is to acknowledge quickly, establish a single owner, and keep partner communication moving without waiting for extra approval.

## Trigger
Start this checklist immediately when any of the following is true:
- A partner reports a service issue, outage, data problem, or degraded experience.
- The growth team becomes aware of an incident that affects a partner-facing workflow, campaign, integration, delivery, reporting, billing, or access.
- It is not yet fully confirmed, but there is a reasonable chance the partner is impacted.

## Owner
- The growth team member covering comms at the time becomes the temporary communication owner immediately.
- That owner is authorized to send the first outbound partner note right away without additional approval.
- If another owner is assigned later, handoff must include current status, what has been sent, and the next promised update time.

## First Outbound Note Deadline
- Send the first outbound note to the affected partner within **30 minutes** of incident discovery or first credible report, whichever comes first.
- This is a firm deadline. Do not wait for root cause, full impact analysis, or leadership approval before acknowledging.
- If details are still limited, send a brief acknowledgement with known impact, current status, and the next update time.

## Immediate Checklist
1. Record the incident start time and the time the team became aware.
2. Identify the affected partner or partner group.
3. Confirm who is the communication owner.
4. Draft and send the first outbound note within 30 minutes.
5. State what is known, what is being investigated, and when the next update will be sent.
6. Open a running internal log of outbound messages, timestamps, and partner replies.
7. Continue updates on the promised cadence until resolution.

## What The First Outbound Note Must Include
- A clear acknowledgement that an incident is affecting the partner or may be affecting them.
- The time the issue was identified or the window in which impact began, if known.
- A plain-language description of the current impact.
- A statement that the team is actively investigating or working the issue.
- A specific next update time.
- A direct contact path for urgent partner follow-up if needed.

## Default First Outbound Note Template
**Subject:** Acknowledgement: Incident affecting [partner name]

Hi [partner name],

We’re reaching out to acknowledge an incident currently affecting [brief description of workflow, integration, campaign, report, or service]. We became aware of this at [time] and are actively investigating.

What we know right now:
- Impact: [plain-language description]
- Status: [investigating / mitigation in progress / monitoring]
- Scope: [what appears affected, if known]

We will send the next update by **[time, timezone]**, even if we are still investigating.

If you have urgent questions in the meantime, reply directly to this message and we will coordinate from there.

Best,  
[Name]  
Growth Team

## Update Cadence
- Promise a concrete next update in every message.
- Default cadence while the incident is active: **every 60 minutes**.
- Use a faster cadence of **every 30 minutes** if the incident is ongoing and materially blocks partner activity.
- Send an update at the promised time even if there is no major change; say so directly.

## Update Content Checklist
Each follow-up update should include:
- Current status
- What changed since the last update
- Current partner impact
- Any workaround, if one exists
- Next update time

## Resolution Note Checklist
When the incident is resolved, send a closing note that includes:
- Confirmation that the incident is resolved
- Resolution time
- Whether any partner action is required
- Whether a follow-up summary will be shared, and when

## Guardrails
- Do not delay acknowledgement while waiting for certainty.
- Do not speculate about root cause if it is not confirmed.
- Do not promise resolution timing unless it is confirmed.
- Keep language factual, plain, and action-oriented.
- If coverage is thin or the primary owner is unavailable, the current comms cover sends the note anyway without extra approval.
