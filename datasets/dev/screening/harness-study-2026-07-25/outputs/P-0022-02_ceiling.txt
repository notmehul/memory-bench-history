# Launch Blurb Support

When the platform team helps with launch blurbs, keep the copy clear, simple, and easy to scan. Use plain language. Cut jargon, internal terms, and technical shorthand unless a term is required for accuracy.

Match an upbeat tone. Sound confident, helpful, and forward-looking without sounding hypey. Focus on what is new, why it matters, and who benefits.

Use this quick check before handing off a draft:
1. Lead with the user-facing value in the first 2 sentences.
2. Keep the blurb to 75 to 120 words unless the request asks for a different length.
3. Include no more than 3 key points.
4. Replace technical detail with outcome-focused language where possible.
5. Read it once for clarity and once for tone before sharing.

If a draft feels dense, rewrite for simpler words, shorter sentences, and a stronger opening.
