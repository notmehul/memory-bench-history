# Platform Weekly Sync Meeting-Notes Checklist

- [ ] Add the meeting date, attendees, and note-taker at the top.
- [ ] Capture each decision as it happens in a dedicated **Decisions** section.
- [ ] For every decision, record:
  - [ ] the decision made
  - [ ] the owner
  - [ ] the due date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
- [ ] Capture action items in a dedicated **Actions** section before the meeting ends.
- [ ] For every action item, record:
  - [ ] the task
  - [ ] the owner
  - [ ] the due date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
- [ ] Link every related ticket in the notes before the meeting ends.
- [ ] If a topic has no ticket yet, note `Ticket needed` next to it before closing the notes.
- [ ] Do a final in-meeting scan to confirm there are no missing owners, due dates, or ticket links.
- [ ] Mark the notes ready to post before the meeting closes.
