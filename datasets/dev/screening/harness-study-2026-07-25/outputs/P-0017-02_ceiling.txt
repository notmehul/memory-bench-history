# Standup Rhythm

Our default standup rhythm is async across the organization, with one shared live sync on Wednesdays. This gives everyone a consistent weekly pattern: lightweight written updates on ordinary days and one real-time gathering to stay aligned together.

## Weekly Pattern

### Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday: Async Standups
On ordinary workdays, standup happens asynchronously.

What to do:
- Post your update in the designated async standup channel or tool for your team or org area.
- Share what you completed, what you are working on next, and any blockers or decisions that need attention.
- Keep it clear and concise so others can scan it quickly.
- Read relevant updates from teammates and respond if you can help unblock something.

What participation looks like:
- Everyone is expected to contribute a written update on async standup days.
- Updates should be posted during your normal workday so teammates have current context.
- If you have no major changes, a short update is still useful.

A simple format:
- Yesterday: what you finished or moved forward
- Today: what you plan to work on
- Blockers: anything slowing you down or needing input

### Wednesday: Shared Live Sync
Wednesday is the one org-wide live standup touchpoint.

What to do:
- Join the live sync on time and come prepared with your current priorities, progress, and blockers.
- Keep updates brief and focused on alignment, dependencies, and anything that benefits from real-time discussion.
- Use the meeting to surface risks, clarify cross-functional questions, and confirm shared priorities for the rest of the week.

What participation looks like:
- Attendance is expected unless you are out of office or have a conflicting priority approved by your manager or team lead.
- Everyone should participate in a way that helps the group stay aligned.
- Topics that require deep problem-solving should usually be taken offline with the relevant people after the sync.

## What Good Standup Participation Looks Like

Across both async and live standups:
- Be consistent.
- Be specific.
- Call out blockers early.
- Focus on work that affects team or org visibility.
- Keep updates short, factual, and easy to act on.

## Quick Reference for New Hires

If you are new, follow this default:
- Monday: post an async update
- Tuesday: post an async update
- Wednesday: join the live sync
- Thursday: post an async update
- Friday: post an async update

When in doubt, share:
- what changed,
- what you are doing next,
- and where you need help.

This is the current standup rhythm across the organization.
