# Meeting Wrap-Up Card

## Share Notes First
**Within 15 minutes of every meeting, send the notes.** If I have back-to-back meetings, send a short version first and finish the full version by the end of the hour.

## Minimum Note Format
- Purpose: one line on what the meeting was for
- Decisions: what was decided
- Actions: owner + due date for every follow-up
- Risks or open questions: what still needs resolution
- Next checkpoint: next meeting, async update, or explicit close

## Before Leaving The Call
- Restate the decision or current direction out loud
- Confirm each action has one owner
- Confirm each action has a concrete due date
- Name the next checkpoint before everyone drops

## Right After The Call
- Post notes in the shared thread, doc, or channel used for the work
- Flag anything time-sensitive at the top
- Convert my own follow-ups into tracked tasks within 30 minutes
- Rename any vague action item until it reads like something that can be done today

## If The Meeting Was Messy
- Send a 5-line summary anyway
- Separate facts from assumptions
- Call out unresolved items explicitly
- Propose the smallest next step with an owner and date

## End-Of-Day Sweep
At 4:30 PM, check that every meeting from the day has:
- notes sent
- actions tracked
- owners confirmed
- due dates assigned
- no orphaned follow-ups in my head or inbox

## Personal Standard
If someone should wonder what happened after the meeting, I wrapped it up too slowly.
