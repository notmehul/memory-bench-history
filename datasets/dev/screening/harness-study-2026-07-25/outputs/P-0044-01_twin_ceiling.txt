- **Decision owner:** Your direct manager decides whether an unusually expensive itinerary is okay to book; Finance does not pre-approve travel.
- **When this step applies:** If the lowest practical itinerary for your trip is more than **$2,000 USD total**, pause and get your manager's approval **before** you book; otherwise, book normally and expense the trip after travel.
- **What to do next:** Send your manager the itinerary link or screenshot, the total cost, and a one-sentence reason it is the best option; once they approve in writing in Slack or email, book it and submit the expense after the trip.
