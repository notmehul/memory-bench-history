## Launch Blurb Support

When the platform team helps with launch blurbs, aim for clear, restrained, formal copy that explains the user-facing value without sounding promotional. Keep the draft grounded in what shipped, who it helps, and why it matters now.

Use this working format:
- one sentence on the launch itself
- one sentence on the practical benefit
- one sentence on any important scope, limit, or availability detail

Keep blurbs between sixty and ninety words. Lead with the outcome, not the implementation. Prefer plain product language over internal terms, architectural detail, or feature-building history. Avoid hype words, unsupported superlatives, and vague claims such as "powerful," "seamless," or "game-changing."

Before handing off a draft, check that it answers three questions:
- What is new?
- Who is it for?
- What changes for the user?

If any of those answers are missing, the blurb is not ready.
