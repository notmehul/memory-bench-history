# Company Standup Routine

Starting now, the whole company will use one shared standup routine every workday.

**When:** Monday to Friday, by **10:30 AM local time**  
**Where:** Post in the **`#daily-standup`** Slack channel  
**Format:** Keep it to **3 short bullets**
- **Yesterday:** what you completed
- **Today:** what you are working on
- **Blockers:** anything slowing you down or needing help

**Guidelines**
- Keep each update to **under 5 lines**
- If you have **no blockers**, write `Blockers: none`
- If you are blocked for **more than 24 hours**, tag the person or team that can help
- If a discussion takes more than **5 back-and-forth messages**, move it to a quick call or thread follow-up

The goal is simple: everyone posts once per workday, everyone can scan progress quickly, and blockers surface early without needing extra manager coordination.
