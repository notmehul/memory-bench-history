## Bug Triage Cadence

Bug triage runs on weekdays except Monday. There is no bug triage session on Mondays.

Each scheduled triage session includes a published owner so everyone can see who is leading that session at a glance. If no owner is published for a session, treat it as unscheduled until ownership is listed.
