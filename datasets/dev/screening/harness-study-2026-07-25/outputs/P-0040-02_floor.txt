# Platform Weekly Sync Notes Checklist

- Fill the header before the meeting starts: `date`, `time`, `facilitator`, `note-taker`, and meeting link.
- Record attendance in the first 5 minutes: `present`, `late`, and `absent`.
- Add each agenda topic as it starts with a timestamp in `HH:MM` and the topic owner.
- For every topic, capture the outcome in 1 to 3 bullets max: decision made, status change, or follow-up needed.
- If a discussion runs more than 5 minutes without a clear outcome, mark it `Open` and note the exact decision still needed.
- For every decision, write:
  - the decision in one sentence
  - who owns it
  - when it takes effect
  - any stated dependency or rollback condition
- For every action item, do not leave the meeting without:
  - one directly responsible owner
  - one concrete due date
  - one clear deliverable
- If no due date is stated for an action item, set it to `before next weekly sync`.
- For every blocker or risk, capture:
  - affected service, system, or workflow
  - severity as `High`, `Medium`, or `Low`
  - current impact
  - current workaround, if any
  - escalation owner
- Move side threads to `Parking Lot` once they stay off-topic for more than 3 minutes.
- Mark any item needing async follow-up with the destination channel, doc, or ticket.
- In the last 5 minutes, read back all decisions and action items and confirm owners live.
- Before the meeting ends, make sure the notes include these final sections:
  - `Decisions`
  - `Action Items`
  - `Blockers/Risks`
  - `Parking Lot`
  - `Next Sync Inputs`
- Do not end the meeting with any action item missing an owner or due date.
- Keep the notes factual and brief; capture commitments, changes, and unresolved issues, not the full debate.
