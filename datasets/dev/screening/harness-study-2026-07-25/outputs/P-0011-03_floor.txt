# Growth Partner Incident Checklist

## Purpose
Use this checklist when a confirmed or likely incident affects an external partner’s traffic, tracking, reporting, payout visibility, campaign delivery, or launch timing. The goal is to keep affected partners informed quickly, clearly, and consistently.

## Roles
Assign these roles immediately:

- `Incident lead`: owns decisions, timeline, and internal coordination for growth.
- `Comms cover`: sends all partner updates and keeps the outbound log current.
- `Partner list owner`: confirms exactly which partners, contacts, and channels are in scope.

One person may cover multiple roles if needed, but `comms cover` must be explicitly named.

## Start Conditions
Start this checklist as soon as any of the following is true:

- A partner-facing workflow is down or materially degraded.
- Tracking, attribution, or reporting is delayed by more than 15 minutes.
- A launch, campaign, or partner deliverable is blocked.
- Three or more partners are likely affected by the same issue.
- One strategic or revenue-critical partner is affected.

Do not wait for full root cause confirmation if partner impact is already likely.

## First 10 Minutes
- Name the `incident lead`, `comms cover`, and `partner list owner`.
- Write a one-line incident summary:
  - what is broken
  - when it started or was first observed
  - who is affected
  - current known workaround, if any
- Build the affected partner list:
  - partner name
  - primary contact
  - backup contact
  - channel to use now (`email`, `Slack`, `shared channel`, or `partner portal`)
  - severity for that partner (`critical`, `high`, `standard`)
- Decide whether the issue is:
  - `critical`: launch-blocking, spend-blocking, payout-blocking, or live revenue impact
  - `high`: meaningful degradation without full outage
  - `standard`: limited or recoverable impact

## First Outbound Note
**Deadline:** send the very first outbound note to every affected partner within **15 minutes of incident confirmation**, or within **5 minutes of taking over comms coverage**, whichever is sooner.

**Approval rule:** the `comms cover` is explicitly authorized to send this first note **without additional approval** from leadership, legal, product, engineering, or the incident lead, as long as the note:
- states only confirmed facts,
- avoids guessing root cause,
- gives the next update time,
- includes a current workaround if one exists.

If facts are still limited, send the note anyway with a short acknowledgment and a next-update commitment.

### First Outbound Note Template
**Subject:** Issue affecting `Partner Name` - investigating

Hi `Name`,

We’re investigating an issue currently affecting `brief impact, e.g. click tracking / reporting / campaign delivery` for `Partner Name`.

What we know now:
- Start time: `time + timezone` or `first observed around time + timezone`
- Impact: `plain-language impact`
- Workaround: `workaround` or `none at the moment`

Our team is actively working this now. We will send the next update by **`time + timezone`** even if we are still investigating.

If you are seeing anything different on your side, reply here and include examples.

Thanks,  
`Comms cover name`  
Growth Team

## Update Cadence
After the first note is sent:

- Send updates every **30 minutes** while the issue is unresolved.
- If impact is `critical`, send updates every **15 minutes**.
- Once the issue is stable and no longer worsening, move to **hourly** updates until fully resolved.
- If there is no new information, still send the scheduled update and say so plainly.

Every update must include:
- current status
- what changed since last note
- partner impact right now
- workaround status
- next update time

## When To Expand Outreach
Add more partners to the notification list immediately if any of the following happens:

- the issue spreads to additional partner accounts
- impact lasts more than **30 minutes**
- expected revenue, spend, or launch impact becomes non-trivial
- a workaround fails or is incomplete
- inbound partner reports suggest the blast radius is larger than first believed

When in doubt, notify.

## Escalation Thresholds For Growth
Mark the incident as `critical` and use 15-minute updates if any of these apply:

- a top-tier or contract-sensitive partner is affected
- paid acquisition or partner-driven revenue is blocked
- attribution or reporting is delayed by more than **60 minutes**
- campaign launch or promised delivery misses its committed start window
- more than **5 partners** are affected
- the incident is visible enough that partners may pause spend or traffic

## Partner Response Handling
- Reply to partner questions within **10 minutes** during the active incident window.
- If a partner asks for details we cannot confirm, say that the issue is under active investigation and commit to the next update time.
- Do not speculate about root cause, blame another team, or promise an exact fix time unless confirmed.
- Log every outbound and inbound partner message in one running incident timeline.

## Resolution Note
Send the resolution note within **30 minutes** of confirmed recovery.

### Resolution Note Template
**Subject:** Resolved: Issue affecting `Partner Name`

Hi `Name`,

The issue affecting `brief impact` has been resolved as of `time + timezone`.

Summary:
- Incident window: `start time` to `resolved time`
- Impact: `plain-language summary`
- Current status: service is back to normal / reporting is catching up / campaign delivery has resumed
- Follow-up: we will share a fuller summary by `day/time` if additional validation is still underway

Please let us know if you continue to see any issues on your side.

Thanks,  
`Comms cover name`  
Growth Team

## Aftercare
Within **1 business day** after resolution:

- send a follow-up summary to affected partners if the incident lasted more than **60 minutes**, affected a strategic partner, or caused a launch/revenue disruption
- confirm whether any backfill, credit, relaunch, or manual cleanup is needed from the growth side
- record:
  - affected partners
  - first outbound sent time
  - whether the 15-minute SLA was met
  - total duration
  - major questions or objections raised by partners
  - any follow-up commitments and owners

## Definition Of Done
This checklist is complete when:

- all affected partners received the first outbound note on time
- scheduled updates were maintained until recovery
- all partner replies were addressed
- the resolution note was sent
- follow-up commitments are recorded with owners and dates
