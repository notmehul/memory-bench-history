# Platform Sprint Boundary Checklist

Use this at sprint close and again before the next sprint is committed whenever there is unfinished estimated work.

- Check remaining work by engineer, not original ticket size.
- Apply the current rule exactly: **carryover is limited to 5 story points per engineer each sprint**.
- If an engineer is at **5 story points or below**, the remaining work can carry as planned.
- If an engineer is **above 5 story points**, the lead must split the leftover work before the next sprint commit.
- Put the carryover estimate only on the unfinished remainder that is still being brought forward.
- Make sure every carried ticket has **one named owner**.
- Keep the carryover portion small enough to fit within the engineer’s `5-point` cap; move the rest into a separate follow-on ticket for later planning.
- In the planning note, record for each carryover item: ticket name, owner, remaining points, and what was split out.

## Worked Example

Engineer `Alex` ends the sprint with **8 story points** of unfinished work left on one story.

Lead action:
- Split the leftover into `5 story points` that will carry into the next sprint.
- Move the remaining `3 story points` into a separate follow-on ticket.

Example split:
- `PLA-214A`: finish retry logic, final tests, and rollout steps — **5 points** — owner: `Alex` — carries into next sprint.
- `PLA-214B`: cleanup work, dashboard follow-up, and non-blocking refactor — **3 points** — owner: `Alex` or reassigned during planning — does **not** count as carryover for the same engineer in the next sprint commit.

Result:
- Alex stays within the current per-engineer carryover limit of **5 story points**.
- The leftover work is still visible, estimated, and schedulable without hiding over-cap carryover inside one oversized story.
