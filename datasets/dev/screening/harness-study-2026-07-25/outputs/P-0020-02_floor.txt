# Growth Ops Checklist: Platform Vendor Bills

## Scope
Use this checklist for Growth-owned platform vendor bills such as ad platforms, analytics tools, outbound tools, creative tooling, data vendors, and other recurring or one-off software charges paid by Growth.

## Routing Rule
- Bills **under `$2,500 USD`** follow the **standard Growth approval path**.
- Bills **at or above `$2,500 USD`** follow the **escalated approval path** and require Finance review before payment is released.
- Treat any invoice marked overdue, prepaid, or service-at-risk as **priority same-day handling** regardless of amount.

## Required Handoff Order Before Funds Are Sent
1. **Growth requestor** confirms the charge is expected and within approved budget.
2. **Growth ops owner** checks invoice accuracy and routing threshold.
3. **Growth manager** approves the spend.
4. **Finance/AP** validates payment details, tax/vendor info, and schedules payment.
5. **Funds are sent** only after Finance/AP confirms all prior handoffs are complete.

## Standard Path: Bills Under $2,500
- [ ] Post the invoice in `#ops` with:
  - vendor name
  - invoice amount in USD
  - due date
  - billing period
  - tool or platform purpose
  - budget owner
- [ ] Confirm the invoice matches the contract, subscription plan, or expected monthly spend.
- [ ] Confirm the vendor, entity name, and payment destination match prior records.
- [ ] Check for duplicate billing, auto-renew overlap, or unauthorized seat expansion.
- [ ] Confirm the spend fits the current Growth budget line.
- [ ] Get approval from the Growth manager in-channel.
- [ ] Hand off to Finance/AP with the approved invoice and payment deadline.
- [ ] Wait for Finance/AP confirmation that payment is scheduled before marking complete.

## Escalated Path: Bills $2,500 And Above
- [ ] Post the invoice in `#ops` with the same details as above plus:
  - reason for spend
  - expected outcome or business need
  - whether this is recurring or one-time
- [ ] Verify contract terms, pricing, renewal date, and any committed minimums.
- [ ] Confirm the spend is budgeted; if not, note the source of coverage.
- [ ] Get approval from the Growth manager.
- [ ] Route to Finance/AP for payment review and release approval.
- [ ] If payment terms changed or bank details changed, require fresh vendor verification before payment.
- [ ] Do not send forward as ready-to-pay until Finance/AP explicitly confirms approval.
- [ ] Mark complete only after Finance/AP confirms payment is scheduled or sent.

## Invoice Checks Every Time
- [ ] Vendor legal name is correct.
- [ ] Invoice number is present.
- [ ] Amount and currency are correct.
- [ ] Due date is clear.
- [ ] Billing period is correct.
- [ ] PO, contract reference, or subscription reference is attached if applicable.
- [ ] No sales tax/VAT issue is unresolved.
- [ ] Payment method on file is still valid.
- [ ] Supporting context exists for unusual overages or one-time fees.

## Timing Expectations
- [ ] Submit invoices to `#ops` within **1 business day** of receipt.
- [ ] Route invoices due within **5 business days** as priority.
- [ ] Escalate same day if service interruption risk is mentioned.
- [ ] Follow up with Finance/AP if no payment scheduling confirmation within **2 business days**.

## Completion Standard
A platform vendor bill is not complete when it is posted or approved. It is complete only when:
- [ ] all required handoffs happened in order, and
- [ ] Finance/AP confirmed payment is scheduled or sent.
